The challenge

Every risk on the floor is connected

Daegu·Gyeongbuk is Korea's manufacturing heartland, but tiny sub-50-staff plants dominate — and three structural gaps feed an invisible causal chain.

🧓

Lost expertise

Veteran workers retire en masse; their feel for a machine's faint warning signs is never passed on.

🌐

Language & skill barrier

Short-term and foreign workers fill the gap — safety rules go unread, human error rises.

🔊

Alarm fatigue

Core technology · H-M Synced

Catch the machine's scream before the worker breaks

A central fusion AI aligns every machine and worker on one timeline. If a rise in the Machine-Stress Index is followed within 30 minutes by a matching rise in the Human-Stress Index, it confirms “human–machine stress synchronization” and intervenes both ways instantly.

M-Index

Machine-Stress Index

Ultrasonic friction amplitude + MEMS vibration deviation, scaled 0–100.

within 30 min
H-Index

Human-Stress Index

HRV LF/HF ratio + EDA change-rate, scaled 0–100.

Dual-Action Intervention

⚙️

Machine side

AI finely decelerates RPM/output within safe limits until parts are replaced — cutting the high-frequency noise and vibration at the source.

🎧

Human side

Active noise-cancelling intercom switches on, and a bone-conduction earphone prescribes a native-language 10-minute refresh break.
