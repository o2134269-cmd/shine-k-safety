The challenge

Two kinds of danger hide on every factory floor

SME manufacturers carry the highest accident burden, yet most rely on experience and paper checklists. Risk lives in two timescales — and both go unmeasured.

⚠️

Accident Hazard

Immediate, process-driven events

  • Caught-in & pinch points on machinery
  • Falls from height & slips
  • Explosion, fire & chemical leaks
  • Struck-by & collisions
60%of 2023 manufacturing accidents were caught-in, falls or struck-by
🫁

Cumulative Hazard

Slow harm from repeated exposure

  • Musculoskeletal strain & back pain
  • Respiratory disease & pneumoconiosis
  • Noise, vibration & heat stress
  • Chronic conditions over years
39%of Gyeongbuk occupational illness is musculoskeletal

80%of fatal accidents at firms under 50 staff
